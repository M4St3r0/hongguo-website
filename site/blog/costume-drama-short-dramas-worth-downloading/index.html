What makes a drama "古装"

古装 combines 古 (ancient) and 装 (costume/attire) — dramas dressed in an imperial or pre-modern Chinese setting rather than a contemporary one. On 红果, that backdrop rarely stands alone; it's usually paired with the same engines that drive the platform's modern-day dramas — a marriage of convenience, a family power struggle, a hidden identity — just relocated to a court, a general's household, or a feudal estate. What holds constant across the genre is the setting itself: titles, ranks, and palace or noble-house politics standing in for the modern workplace or family boardroom.
