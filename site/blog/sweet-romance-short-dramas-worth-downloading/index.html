What makes a drama "甜宠"

甜宠 combines 甜 (sweet) and 宠 (doted-on/pampered), and it names a whole approach to romance rather than one plot: instead of building tension through betrayal, misunderstanding, or a slow-burn standoff, the story leans into a lead — usually the male lead — who is openly, unashamedly devoted to the heroine almost from the start. Conflict, when it shows up, tends to come from outside the relationship (family disapproval, a rival, a secret identity) rather than between the two leads themselves. It's the genre people reach for when they want the reassurance of a doting partner without the emotional whiplash of a revenge or rebirth plot — several of the titles below pair that sweetness with a specific hook (a secret crush, a marriage of convenience, a palace setting), which is standard variety within the genre rather than an exception to it.
