What happens to your downloads if a drama gets pulled from the app

By M4St3r0·Updated 3 September 2026·~5 min read
Short answer

Nothing happens to episodes you've already downloaded. They're plain 1080p .mp4 files sitting on your own PC — the app doesn't phone home to 红果 to keep playing them, so a title being pulled from the platform has no effect on files you already have. The only real gap is episodes you hadn't downloaded yet when a series got pulled — those can't be fetched anymore, since the source is gone. Since v1.0.0.42, trying to queue a removed title now shows an instant "Unavailable (已下架)" toast, so you find out immediately instead of watching a download quietly fail.

This is one of the most common worries people bring to Hongguo Downloader before their first download: "if this drama disappears later, do I lose what I've saved?" It's a fair question — streaming apps train you to expect that anything you're watching can vanish without warning. The answer here is reassuring, but it comes with one honest caveat worth understanding up front.

The short version: your downloads are already yours

Once Hongguo Downloader finishes saving an episode, it's a normal .mp4 file in a folder you chose, plus an entry in the app's Library. Nothing about that file depends on 红果 still hosting the drama — it's not a streaming license, a temporary cache, or a link that expires. It plays with any video player, it doesn't check for permission before playing, and moving it to another drive or PC works exactly like moving any other video file. If 红果 removes the title tomorrow, the file on your computer doesn't know or care.

What actually changes when a title is pulled

Two things, and only two:

  • You can no longer re-download it. If you deleted the file, or never finished downloading the full series, the app can't fetch what's no longer on the platform. The source is gone, not just hidden.
  • "Update all" can't pull new episodes for an ongoing series. If you were following a still-releasing show and it gets pulled mid-run, whatever episodes had already aired and downloaded are yours to keep — but any episodes that hadn't released yet, obviously, were never coming.

Everything else — playback, your Library entry, the file's location on disk, moving it to a new PC — is completely unaffected. See moving your downloaded dramas to a new PC, phone, or drive if you want the details on that.

The one real risk — an ongoing series pulled mid-run

The scenario worth actually planning around isn't a complete, already-finished series — those you either have in full or you don't. It's a series that's still releasing (连载中) when it gets pulled. In that case you keep everything that had aired and downloaded by that point, but the ending is simply never coming, because the platform itself no longer has it. This is uncommon, but it's the reason it's worth downloading episodes of an ongoing favorite as they release rather than waiting for the finale to grab everything at once — see the Update all tip in how to keep a 红果 drama after it disappears for the habit that avoids this entirely.

How v1.0.0.42 changed this: instant Unavailable flagging

Earlier versions of the app would just fail or hang if you tried to queue a title that 红果 no longer had. As of v1.0.0.42, the Library and queue now flag a removed title the moment you try to add it, with a direct "Unavailable (已下架)" toast — 已下架 literally means "taken off the shelf," the same term 红果 itself uses internally for delisted titles. You find out immediately, instead of wondering whether the download is just slow.

This same flag also shows up for titles already sitting in your Library if you try to re-queue or update them after removal — so you always know exactly which of your saved titles are still fetchable from the source and which are frozen at whatever you already have.

What to do if you think a title might get pulled soon

There's no public warning before a removal — it's driven by licensing, not by anything visible in the app ahead of time. The practical habit that covers you either way: download a drama you actually care about while it's available, rather than leaving it in a watch-later list. Frequently asked questions

Do my already-downloaded episodes stop working if a drama is removed?

No. They're plain .mp4 files on your own PC that don't check back with 红果 to keep playing — removal from the platform has zero effect on files you already have.

What if I was only partway through downloading when it got pulled?

You keep whatever finished downloading before removal. Episodes that hadn't downloaded yet can't be fetched anymore, since the source is gone.

How does the app know a title has been pulled?

Since v1.0.0.42, queuing a removed title shows an instant "Unavailable (已下架)" toast right away, instead of the download quietly failing or hanging.

Can the app restore a title after it's fully removed?

No — it can only pull from what's currently on 红果. Once a title is completely gone from the source, there's nothing left to fetch.
