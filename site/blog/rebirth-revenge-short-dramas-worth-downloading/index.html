What makes a drama "重生"

重生 literally means "reborn," and it covers any story where the lead comes back — after death, after ruin, after being betrayed — to an earlier moment in their own timeline, carrying full memory of everything that happened the first time around. That foreknowledge is the whole engine of the genre: the lead already knows which "friend" will betray them, which decision led to disaster, which insult will be repaid tenfold. It pairs constantly with 逆袭 (turning the tables from underdog to winner) and 复仇 (revenge), which is exactly why the two tags show up together across most of the catalogue — several of the titles below stack rebirth with a specific decade setting or a stated foe, which is standard for the genre rather than an unusual twist.
