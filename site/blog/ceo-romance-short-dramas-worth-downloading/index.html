What makes a drama "霸总"

霸总 is short for 霸道总裁 — literally "domineering president/CEO." The male lead runs a company, a family fortune, or both; he's blunt, controlling in small ways, and exclusively devoted to the female lead once the plot locks them together. It pairs constantly with tropes like flash marriage, hidden identity, and rags-to-riches reversals — several of the titles below combine two or three of those at once, which is normal for the genre rather than unusual.
