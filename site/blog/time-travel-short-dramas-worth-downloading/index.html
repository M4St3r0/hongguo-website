What makes a drama "穿越"

穿越 literally means "crossing through," and it's the umbrella term for any story where the lead is transported out of their original time and place — most often backward into a past era, but sometimes into another body, another identity, or another world entirely. A common subtype, 穿书 ("transmigrated into a book"), sends the lead into the body of a character from a novel they were reading, complete with the plot they already know is coming. It pairs constantly with survival, rebirth, and romance tropes — several of the titles below combine 穿越 with a specific decade setting or a second twist, which is normal for the genre rather than unusual.
