Short answer

To save a 红果 (Hongguo) short-drama to your computer, install the free Hongguo Downloader app for Windows, search for the drama (or paste its share link), pick the episodes and quality, and press Download. Every episode is saved to a folder you choose as a normal .mp4 file you can watch offline forever — with no Android emulator, no login, and no monthly fee.

Short-dramas move fast. A series you love can climb the charts one week and quietly disappear the next — or sit behind a region lock, a login, or a spotty connection when you actually want to watch. The reliable fix is to keep your own copy on your PC. Here is exactly how to do that, step by step.

What you'll need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC
  • The free Hongguo Downloader installer (about 84 MB) — download it here
  • The title of the drama you want, or a 红果 share link

That's it. There is no account to make, no Android emulator to install, and no Python or Java to set up first.

Download a Hongguo drama in 4 steps

Step 1 — Install Hongguo Downloader

Download the installer from the official download button and run it. It installs per-user, so it doesn't ask for admin rights, and it bundles everything it needs. When it opens, you'll land on a Trending wall of dramas — no sign-in required.

Step 2 — Find the drama you want

There are three easy ways to find something:

  • Search by title — type the name into the search box to look through Hongguo's catalogue.
  • Paste a share link — in the 红果 app, open a drama, tap Share, copy the link, and paste it in. It resolves instantly.
  • Browse Trending — flick through the Recommended, Hot and New boards if you're not sure what to watch.

This works for classic short-dramas and for the new wave of AI-generated dramas (AI 短剧) alike — if it's on 红果, you can grab it.

Step 3 — Choose your episodes and quality

Open the drama and use the per-episode picker. You can take the whole series, a handful, or a specific range like 1‑3, 5, 8‑12. Set the quality up to full 1080p, and choose how many series run at once and how fast to go.

Step 4 — Download and keep it forever

Press Download. A live progress queue shows each episode as it lands. Every episode is saved as a clean, playable file named 第NNN集.mp4 — no converting, no ffmpeg, nothing to untangle.

Where your files go

Into a folder you choose (your Videos folder by default). Each drama gets its own folder full of .mp4 episodes you fully own and can play in any video app, offline, forever.

How to grab a whole series (or several) at once

You don't have to babysit downloads one at a time. Tap the posters of as many dramas as you like to line them up, then let the queue run. Each series files itself into its own folder as it finishes — perfect for building a library overnight.

Keep your library up to date automatically

Everything you download appears as a poster-wall Library you can sort by newest, rating, or recently added. When a series you follow releases more episodes, Hongguo Downloader spots them and shows a “+N new” badge. One tap on Update all fetches only the missing episodes — it never re-downloads what you already have.
