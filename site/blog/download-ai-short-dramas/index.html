What is an AI short-drama (AI 短剧)?

An AI 短剧 (sometimes AI 微短剧) is a short-drama produced with generative AI doing a lot of the heavy lifting — AI-generated or AI-assisted visuals, AI voices, and in some cases whole AI-built scenes and characters. The format is the same as any other duanju: vertical, phone-first, 1–2 minute episodes, a full story told across dozens of them.

Why are AI short-dramas blowing up?

  • Speed and cost. AI tools let a tiny team produce a full series far faster and cheaper than a traditional shoot.
  • Volume. More titles ship every week, which means more variety — and more experiments — for viewers.
  • Novelty. AI makes premises possible (fantasy worlds, impossible settings) that would be expensive to film for real.
