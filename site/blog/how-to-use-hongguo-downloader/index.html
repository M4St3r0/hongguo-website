How to use Hongguo Downloader: a complete walkthrough

By M4St3r0·Updated 21 August 2026·~6 min read
Short answer

Using Hongguo Downloader takes about a minute: install the free Windows app, search a title or paste a 红果 / hongguoduanju link, pick your episodes, and press Download. Every episode saves as a plain 1080p .mp4 in a folder you choose — no emulator, no login. This walkthrough covers each screen of the Hongguo video downloader, from install to keeping your library current.

Whether you found this looking for a Chinese short drama downloader, a hongguoduanju video downloader, or just a way to keep your 红果短剧 offline, the app is built to be obvious. Here's every step, in order.

Step 1 — Install Hongguo Downloader

Grab the installer from the official download button (about 84 MB) and run it. It's a per-user install, so it won't ask for admin rights, and it bundles everything — there's no Python, Java, or Android emulator to set up. If you already have it and a new version exists, the installer lets you choose a quick Update or a full Clean Install, and either way your license and downloaded library are kept. When it opens, you land straight on a Trending wall — no sign-in.

Step 2 — Find a drama (three ways)

This is the heart of any duanju downloader. You have three options:

  1. Search by title — type the drama's name in the search box to look through the 红果 catalogue. Results show posters, ratings, and episode counts.
  2. Paste a share link — in the 红果 app, open a drama, tap Share, and copy the link (a hongguoduanju.com or novelquickapp.com link both work). Paste it and press Go; it resolves instantly.
  3. Browse Trending — flip through Recommended, Hot, and New if you're not sure what to grab.

All three work for classic short-dramas and for AI-generated dramas (AI 短剧) — so it doubles as an AI drama downloader without any mode-switching.

Step 3 — Add dramas to your queue

Tap a poster (or the result card) to add a drama to Your queue. You can line up as many as you like — this is what makes it a real batch short drama downloader rather than a one-at-a-time tool. Each queued series shows its title and episode count so you know exactly what's about to download.

Step 4 — Pick episodes and quality

Before you download, open a queued series and use the per-episode picker. Take the whole series, or select a range like 1‑3, 5, 8‑12 — perfect for topping up the episodes you missed. Set the quality up to full 1080p, and adjust how many series run at once and how fast the queue goes. It's a true 1080p drama downloader, so you're not stuck with a low-res copy.

Step 5 — Download and watch offline

Press Download. A live progress bar fills for each episode as it lands. When it's done, every episode is a clean, playable file named 第NNN集.mp4 in a folder you chose (your Videos folder by default) — no converting, no ffmpeg. That's your offline short drama copy, ready to play in any video app, forever.

Where your files go

Each drama gets its own folder of .mp4 episodes under the output folder you set. They're plain files you fully own — copy them to a USB stick, a NAS, or another PC and they just play.

Step 6 — Keep your Library up to date

Everything you download shows up in the Library as a poster wall you can sort by newest, rating, or recently added. When a series you follow releases more episodes, you'll see a “+N new” badge. Tap Update all and the app fetches only the missing episodes — it never re-downloads what you already have. This is the part that makes it a keeper as a long-term Chinese video downloader.

A few tips

  • Paste links straight from the 红果 share sheet — it's faster than searching for long titles.
  • Queue several series before bed and let the batch run overnight.
  • On the free plan you get 2 dramas a day; a one-time key removes the limit if you're building a big library.

Frequently asked questions

How do I paste a 红果 link into Hongguo Downloader?

In the 红果 app open a drama, tap Share, copy the link, then paste it into the search bar in Hongguo Downloader and press Go. Both hongguoduanju.com and novelquickapp.com links resolve.

Can I download only some episodes?

Yes. Use the per-episode picker to take a range like 1‑3, 5, 8‑12 instead of the whole series.

Where are the downloaded files saved?

In a folder you choose (Videos by default). Each drama gets its own folder of .mp4 episodes named 第NNN集.mp4.

How do I get new episodes of a series I already downloaded?

Open the Library and tap Update all. It fetches only the missing episodes and skips what you already have.

Do I need an account or emulator?

No. There's no login and no Android emulator — it's a standalone Windows 10 / 11 app.
