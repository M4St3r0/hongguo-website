What FlexTV actually is

FlexTV belongs to the same wave of mobile short-drama apps as ReelShort, GoodShort, NetShort, and ShortMax — a library of vertical, bite-sized dramas designed to be watched in-app, with episodes unlocked through coins or a subscription. Like the rest of that category, it's built as a streaming product: there's no built-in way to export an episode as a video file you keep once you close the app. That's typical of the category, not a shortcoming unique to FlexTV — subscription streaming apps are designed to keep viewing inside them.

What Hongguo Downloader actually is

Hongguo Downloader doesn't try to be a general short-drama app at all. It does one narrow thing: pull dramas from the 红果 (Hongguo) catalogue — the Chinese-language short-dramas and the newer wave of AI-generated dramas — and save them as real, full 1080p .mp4 files on your PC, a whole series in one batch, with no login and no emulator. Two series a day are free; a key removes the daily limit. It's an independent tool, not affiliated with 红果, FlexTV, or any other app.

Do the catalogues overlap?

No. Hongguo Downloader cannot download FlexTV's shows, and FlexTV has no feature that would give you access to 红果's catalogue either. If you're already watching and enjoying FlexTV, this tool won't add anything there — it's built around a completely different library. If what you actually want is to keep 红果 dramas as files instead of renting access to them episode by episode, that's the one thing Hongguo Downloader is for.
