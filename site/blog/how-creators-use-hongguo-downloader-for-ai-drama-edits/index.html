The Problem with Traditional Screen Recording

Before specialized software existed, creators recorded their phone or computer screens while letting drama episodes play out one by one. This caused severe workflow friction:

  • Time Waste: A 90-episode series took 2.5 hours just to record, tying up the computer.
  • Platform Re-compression Blur: Screen captures have low bitrates. When re-uploaded to YouTube Shorts or TikTok, the platform compression algorithm blurs the video into pixelated mush.
  • UI Artifacts: Progress bars, play icons, and watermark overlays had to be cropped out, ruining the vertical framing.
