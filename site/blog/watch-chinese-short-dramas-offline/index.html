What are duanju (Chinese short dramas)?

Duanju (短剧), also called micro-dramas or 微短剧, are vertical, phone-first series with 1–2 minute episodes and anywhere from 20 to 100 episodes per story. A whole series adds up to about a movie or two. They're fast, cliff-hanger-driven, and hugely popular — with apps like 红果 (Hongguo), DramaBox and ReelShort leading the wave.

Why watch them offline?

  • No buffering on the commute, a flight, or a weak connection.
  • Shows get removed. Dramas rotate off apps when licences change — an offline copy survives that.
  • Save mobile data — download once on Wi-Fi, watch as many times as you like.
  • Watch anywhere — copy the files to a phone, tablet, USB stick or TV.
