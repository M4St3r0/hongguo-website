By M4St3r0·Updated 26 August 2026·~6 min read
Short answer

These aren't really competitors — they're two different short-drama worlds. ReelShort is a mobile app for mostly English-language, Western-produced vertical dramas, paid for with coins or a subscription and watched entirely inside the app. Hongguo Downloader is a Windows tool built for one specific catalogue — 红果 (Hongguo)'s Chinese short-dramas and AI-generated dramas — and its whole job is turning them into real .mp4 files you own. Neither one can do the other's job.

People searching "X vs Y" in the short-drama space are usually comparing apples to apples — two apps with the same kind of catalogue. ReelShort and Hongguo Downloader aren't that, and it's worth being upfront about why before you go looking for a feature that isn't there.

What ReelShort actually is

ReelShort, from Crazy Maple Studio, is one of the biggest names in the short-drama boom — a huge, fast-growing library of bite-sized vertical dramas, most of them produced in English for a Western audience (CEO romances, revenge plots, supernatural romance are its signature genres). It's a polished, purely in-app streaming experience: episodes unlock with coins or a subscription, and there's no feature for exporting an episode as a file you keep outside the app. That's a deliberate product choice, not an oversight — streaming apps are built to keep you inside them.

What Hongguo Downloader actually is

Hongguo Downloader doesn't try to be a general short-drama app at all. It does one narrow thing: pull dramas from the 红果 (Hongguo) catalogue — the Chinese-language short-dramas and the newer wave of AI-generated dramas — and save them as real, full 1080p .mp4 files on your PC, a whole series in one batch, with no login and no emulator. Two series a day are free; a key removes the daily limit. It's an independent tool, not affiliated with 红果, ReelShort, or Crazy Maple Studio.

Do the catalogues overlap?

No, and it's worth being direct about that: Hongguo Downloader cannot download ReelShort's shows, and there's no ReelShort feature that could give you Hongguo's catalogue either. If you're already watching and enjoying ReelShort, this tool won't add anything for you — it's simply built around a different library. If what you actually want is to keep 红果 dramas as files instead of renting access to them, that's the one thing it's for.
