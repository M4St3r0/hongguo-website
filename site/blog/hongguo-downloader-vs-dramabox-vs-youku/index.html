DramaBox

DramaBox is one of the biggest overseas duanju apps — a large, well-subtitled catalogue and a slick mobile experience. The trade-off is the model: it's freemium with coins, so unlocking episodes can cost, and its "download" is an in-app offline mode, not exportable video. Great for discovery and binge-watching; not a way to own files.

YOUKU

YOUKU is a major Chinese streaming platform with a massive library that reaches well beyond short-dramas, multi-platform apps, and in-app offline downloads. But those downloads live inside the YOUKU app, tied to your account and subscription with DRM — so they can expire, and they aren't plain files you keep. Excellent for watching broadly; not for archiving.
